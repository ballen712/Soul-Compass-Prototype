## Need help?

Email **soulcompass.support@gmail.com**.

To help us investigate, include:

- Your app version.
- Your phone model and iOS/Android version.
- The screen or feature you were using.
- The steps that led to the issue and the exact error message.
- A screenshot only after hiding birth details, city, collection and other private information.

**Please do not email your birth date, birth time, birthplace, chart, or a screenshot showing your profile.** Support does not need those details for a normal technical report.

## Current app behavior

- No account or password is required.
- Your profile and crystal collection are stored on your device.
- An optional natal-chart calculation sends required birth details to the Soul Compass API only after you grant permission.
- City search sends the city text you enter to the service for matching; it does not use device GPS.
- To erase your saved profile on this device, open Settings → Privacy → Delete local profile and confirm. You will need to select a language and enter your details again. Device backups and technical records created by network features are not erased by this action.

## Basic troubleshooting

1. Check that your device has an internet connection for city search and a new natal-chart calculation.
2. Reopen the app and retry the action once.
3. If chart consent was withdrawn, review the permission in Settings before requesting another calculation.
4. If the issue persists, email the app version, device/OS version and redacted steps.

No response-time guarantee is included until the operator chooses and can meet one.
