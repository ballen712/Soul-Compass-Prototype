---
name: Launch completion boundaries
description: Product-owner constraints for finishing Soul Compass without expanding scope.
---

Finish important existing product gaps and preserve the Soul Compass design identity rather than adding unrelated features or endlessly refactoring.

**Why:** the user explicitly required a smaller set of reliable, useful features in the final product-completion brief.

**How to apply:** implement safe scoped corrections, but request approval before major new functionality, paid services, architecture changes, data deletion or external-infrastructure changes. Do not publish or incur costs without explicit approval. Keep traditional astrology/crystal meanings separate from scientifically established facts, and never present sample content or incomplete translations as finished personalized functionality.

Keep Cosmos copy concise—one reflection heading, one short daily message, then “A moment to keep”—but keep the page complete and intentional rather than visually stripped down. Let the selected daily feeling, available transit theme, and saved intentions inform crystal suggestions; describe stones as symbolic prompts, not as having guaranteed effects.

**Why:** the user asked for daily feeling states to affect crystal picks alongside transits, while keeping the reading simple and grounded.

**How to apply:** keep mood, chart theme, and optional intentions distinct in the recommendation logic and explanation; never promise that a crystal changes a person's health or circumstances.

Soul Compass is a spiritual self-exploration app for everyday users, including people who are not especially spiritual. Keep its language simple, welcoming, and practical.

**Why:** the user described Soul Compass as a spiritual app for ordinary people to explore themselves, with wording that is easy to understand.

**How to apply:** explain symbolic readings in grounded language; avoid assuming spiritual beliefs or presenting astrology and rituals as predictions or guaranteed effects.

Crystal descriptions should directly say what qualities a stone is good for. Do not add visible caveats that crystal meanings are symbolic prompts or unproven effects.

**Why:** the user explicitly asked for direct, benefit-oriented crystal descriptions without that disclaimer.

**How to apply:** use simple language tied to the stone’s guide themes and chart context; keep the copy out of clinical or guaranteed-result claims.

For daily crystal picks, one shared chart theme is enough when no stronger match is available; do not select a stone with no relevant theme connection.

**Why:** the user chose a one-theme fallback to avoid empty daily picks without showing unrelated stones.

**How to apply:** keep the no-match state when there is no personal chart or feeling context, or when all matching stones are hidden.

Natal Chart is an optional, locally saved deep dive, not a prerequisite for Cosmos. Explain its terms in everyday language for people with no astrology background. Planet interpretations must connect the actual planet, sign, and reliable house placement using authored, plain-language copy rather than generic filler.

**Why:** the user wants the daily reflection to remain the main entry point, asked for accessible chart explanations, and specifically rejected machine-like generic descriptions of planet placements.

**How to apply:** define planets, signs, houses, angles, aspects and chart methods plainly. For each placement, explain the planet's everyday theme, the sign's style, the house's life area, then connect all three with a concrete everyday example. Use only calculated chart fields; never infer houses or angles for unknown birth times or choose a single Moon sign when the range is uncertain. Frame interpretations as reflection, not prediction or a fixed verdict.

Major-aspect explanations should connect both actual planetary placements to how the aspect may feel or show up in everyday life.

**Why:** the user asked for Sun–Mars and other aspect descriptions to feel specifically about the reader, not like general definitions followed by a reflection question.

**How to apply:** use each planet's calculated sign and available house, explain the type of connection in plain language, and include a concrete hypothetical example. For unknown birth times, label reference-time aspects as uncertain and omit unverified signs or houses.

Planet explanations should end with one or two clearly hypothetical, everyday examples in place of a reflection question. Use “A hypothetical example” as the heading and begin the next line directly with the scenario, without repeating “For example.”

**Why:** the user wants readers to see how a planet-sign-house combination could show up in ordinary life, without presenting it as a fact about them.

**How to apply:** label examples as hypothetical, use the actual known placement context, and preserve uncertainty when birth time or house placement is unavailable.

The Cosmos tab should use a star-only night sky with no moon, extending to the top screen edge and continuing across the full near-black background. Keep the blue reading cards distinct and readable. Move “A Daily Pause” closer to the Soul Compass brand.

**Why:** the user asked for a centered, full-bleed cosmic header and concise daily guidance that is easy to understand.

**How to apply:** keep the star field subtle around text, fade the top image just below “Today’s reflection,” and phrase focus/caution as reflective suggestions rather than predictions.

Use the same cosmic starfield, dark-blue cards, wordmark, and typography across Cosmos and Natal Chart. Keep Crystals’ outer background warm beige, but reuse the shared typography and cosmic card accents so it still feels like the same app. Keep the wordmark, settings control, page-title baseline, horizontal gutter, and subtitle spacing consistent across all three tabs.

**Why:** the user asked for the three tabs to feel consistent while explicitly keeping Crystals beige, then requested pixel-aligned wordmarks, headings, subtitles, and settings icons.

**How to apply:** use the shared tab header and layout tokens, align screen headings, wordmark, font scale, card shapes, and blue/gold accents; do not add an unrelated decorative image to Crystals.

Cosmos overview, focus, caution, and reflection should offer complementary perspectives, not repeat one instruction in different words. Small ritual ideas should change daily; repetition after some time is acceptable.

**Why:** the user asked for different angles on the day's theme and fresh, simple daily rituals.

**How to apply:** separate the overview, practical opportunity, boundary, and reflective question. Keep a day's ritual choices stable across repeated openings rather than reshuffling on every tap.

Crystal suggestions must draw from a broad catalog while remaining relevant to the chart theme and today's chosen feeling. Daily rotation can distinguish similarly relevant choices; it should not replace relevance.

**Why:** the user found that changing birth details still produced only the same small set of stones.

**How to apply:** preserve meaningful contextual ranking and hidden-stone exclusions when expanding or rotating recommendations.

Daily crystal recommendations are optional: show a main stone and only genuinely matching alternatives, or no stones when nothing fits. Never fill empty results with sample stones or force three cards. An empty day reflects missing relevance, not a claim that a stone is harmful or unsuitable medically.

**Why:** the user explicitly requested days without stones rather than showing unrelated recommendations.

**How to apply:** allow zero to three daily suggestions, preserve today's explicit feeling matches, exclude hidden stones, and keep the separate natal shortlist from becoming a daily fallback. Do not randomly suppress valid recommendations to manufacture empty days.

The daily feeling is optional and resets at the next calendar day on the device, not after 24 hours. Without a choice, use ordinary chart-based guidance. Remove the separate Intentions experience and ignore previously saved intentions.

**Why:** the user said yesterday's mood should not carry into a different day's energy and removed Intentions to avoid duplicating the feeling choice.

**How to apply:** preserve today's mood across same-day restarts, expire it on startup, local midnight and foreground return, and leave birth details, collection, language and hidden stones unchanged. Undated legacy moods cannot be assumed to belong to today.

Birth-chart stone pairings should use reliable natal planet-sign elements, remain a local calculation, and explain why each stone appears. The guide's stone-to-element links are Soul Compass's symbolic editorial system, not proven balancing effects or medical advice. Unknown birth times must omit sign placements the existing chart engine considers uncertain.

**Why:** the user requested a 5–10-stone natal-chart shortlist with a readable reason for each choice and a way to add meaningful stones to a personal collection.

**How to apply:** keep the shortlist separate from daily-transit picks, honor hidden-stone settings, preserve the engine's timed/untimed precision, and never send profile data to a new service for these explanations.

Never show one stone's photograph under another stone's name. Use clearly identified symbolic artwork until a correctly identified, appropriately licensed image is available.

**Why:** the guide supports collecting and shopping, so misleading identification and unverified image rights would undermine its purpose.

**How to apply:** distinguish symbolic illustration from a specimen photograph and verify both identity and usage rights before adding photos.

Every catalog stone should have its own recognizable image in one consistent visual style. Daily explanations should name the actual natal/transit connection or today's chosen feeling behind that stone and explain a concrete everyday use in simple language, not repeat generic traditional-association boilerplate.

**Why:** the user requested images across the whole catalog and explanations of why each stone was chosen for that person today.

**How to apply:** trace each explanation to the candidate's own matching inputs; do not borrow the primary stone's reason for alternatives, invent chart aspects, or promise that a crystal itself increases energy or productivity. Present practical support as a personal reminder or ritual.

Keep small stars visible across the full Cosmos background and scroll area. Use one information symbol for expandable explanations; remove decorative footer icons and keep explanatory dialogs free of redundant headings or repeated questions. The header should show only “SOUL COMPASS” and “Today’s reflection,” without the compass mark, “DAILY PRACTICE,” or “A DAILY PAUSE.” End the page at the Natal Chart card and prevent overscrolling into empty space.

**Why:** the user asked for a consistent, quiet visual language and immediate, more detailed explanations when someone chooses to read further.

**How to apply:** preserve the blue cards, keep the stars low-contrast, and make the Natal Chart card the clear end of the content. Keep the full-screen star layer outside the ScrollView; a tall decorative child can extend the scrollable area and reveal an empty tail.

The Soul Compass voice should be warm, gently mystical, psychologically reflective, and occasionally lightly humorous. Write original copy rather than imitating or quoting a specific teacher.

**Why:** the user asked for a subtle Osho-inspired warmth while keeping the app's wording clear and human.

**How to apply:** use broad traits such as self-inquiry, directness, gentle paradox, and light humor. Keep astrology and crystal associations symbolic, avoid diagnoses or promises, and do not copy distinctive phrasing.
