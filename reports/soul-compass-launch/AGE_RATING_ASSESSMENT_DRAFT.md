# Soul Compass — preliminary App Store age-rating assessment

**Status:** preliminary content review only; not an Apple rating or legal opinion. Complete Apple's current age-rating questionnaire for the final binary before submission.

## Initial recommendation

A low content rating, with **4+ as a candidate to assess**, appears plausible from the features and copy reviewed. This is not confirmed: Apple's current questionnaire and rating result determine the submitted rating.

The product's previously selected **13+ intended audience** is a separate product/legal statement. A possible 4+ store content rating would not mean the app is designed or marketed to young children. Do not change the 13+ wording in the legal drafts based on this preliminary assessment alone.

## Content reviewed

- Daily astrology reflections and a natal-chart explainer.
- Crystal descriptions, suggestions, collection and hidden-stone controls.
- User-selected daily feelings and general self-reflection questions.
- Explore screen currently labels Tarot, longer-term transits and Life Directions as planned; those features are not active.
- No user-to-user messaging, user-generated public content, advertising or in-app purchase flow was found in the active app screens reviewed.
- A keyword scan of the app's visible source text did not find sexual content, nudity, violence, weapons, alcohol/drug promotion, gambling or profanity. This is a source-text scan, not a complete review of every bundled image or final binary.
