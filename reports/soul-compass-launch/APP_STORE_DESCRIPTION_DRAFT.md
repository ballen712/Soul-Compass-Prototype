## Description

**A little more connected to yourself, every day.**

Soul Compass brings daily astrology, self-reflection and the world of crystals together in a calm, easy-to-use space.

Your birth chart is unique. If you choose to create it, Soul Compass looks at your chart alongside current planetary movements and offers a personal theme to reflect on—not the same horoscope for everyone.

**A DAILY MOMENT TO REFLECT**

Read a clear daily message, find a focus for today, and consider what can wait. The language is made for everyday readers, not just astrology experts.

**EXPLORE YOUR NATAL CHART**

Learn about your planets, signs, houses and major aspects through plain-language explanations and clearly hypothetical examples. Details that depend on an exact birth time are shown only when the chart can support them.

**FIND YOUR CRYSTAL COMPANION**

Browse a guide to crystals and their traditional associations. Daily suggestions draw on available chart themes and the feeling you choose. Keep track of stones you own, or hide stones you would rather not see.

**A QUIET SPACE, YOUR WAY**

Choose light or dark appearance, explore in seven available app languages, and return whenever you want a fresh perspective.

No account is required. Your profile, preferences and crystal collection stay on your device. With your permission, birth details are sent to the Soul Compass API to calculate your natal chart. City search sends the place name you type so the app can find a matching location.

Astrology and crystal associations are offered for personal reflection. They are not guaranteed predictions or professional medical, mental-health, legal or financial advice.

**Find your direction. Follow your own path.**
