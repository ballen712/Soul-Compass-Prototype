2. Technical problems found and corrected

  1. Expo Doctor 19/20: the standard SDK-compatible package alignment succeeded after the existing 24-hour release-age window elapsed. Doctor now reports 20/20 and install --check reports up to date. No major SDK upgrade, ignored check or safety exception.
  2. Stale daily content/date: profile-only memoization kept results unchanged indefinitely. Added clock/foreground refresh and a bounded, device-memory-only minute cache.
  3. Saved intentions ignored: recognized intentions now influence ranking; intention-only results are identified separately. Unmapped words are not falsely interpreted.
  4. Hidden primary left no replacement: filtering now precedes ranking; visible alternatives are promoted, with an honest all-hidden recovery state.
  5. Stale asynchronous city results: editing/new searches cancel older requests; generation checks discard responses even if cancellation is ignored. Unmount aborts, timeout is bounded and invalid coordinates/provider shapes are rejected.
  6. Known-time note contradicted full Natal: daily precision copy now distinguishes its planet-only scope from full Natal houses/angles.
  7. Conjunction treated like generic difficulty: reflective copy now distinguishes conjunction, sextile, square, trine and opposition with specific actions, not prediction.
  8. Confusing language/intentions/privacy copy: English-only availability, real recommendation inputs and local versus server calculation pathways are explicit.
  9. Build memory pressure: a simultaneous native/browser verification caused a recorded out-of-memory kill. Metro transform concurrency is now limited to one worker; remaining production builds are run separately.

3. Product and journey improvements

The welcome message explains what the product offers and that time may be unknown. Residence remains optional and never substitutes birthplace. COSMOS points users without residence to the setting needed for local daily guidance. Calculated reflections explain the actual factor and give a practical question; crystal picks explain their traditional association and recognized intention, whether or not owned. Natal keeps technical results but offers a collapsed beginner guide. Loading/error/empty/retry states have explicit labels. The established visual identity and three tabs remain.

Future localization should begin by extracting reviewed English strings behind one small translation interface only when a second launch language is actually approved. Preserve preference codes now; do not add an unreviewed translation framework or claim the stored preference changes the interface.

5. Remaining incomplete / demonstration scope

  • Unavailable daily charts retain clearly marked sample copy, not a personalized fallback.
  • Personal interpretation is a small deterministic template library around one factor, not an extensive natal/transit interpretation product. Crystal selection is a transparent three-stone symbolic rule set.
  • No export/import, app-managed backup or synchronization; device/browser data removal may cause permanent loss. OS backups may include application data.
  • No reviewed additional languages, store listing submission, final legal documents, hosted policy URL, signed native release or physical-device validation.
  • App/browser cold start without downloaded assets is not guaranteed offline. Local editing works after assets are loaded; network charts and city search require connectivity.

9. Privacy policy and terms preparation

This checklist is not a final legal policy. The owner-supplied support contact is available, but the legal operator and deployment retention details are not established here.

  • Local data categories: birth date/time, birthplace/residence, language preference, collection, intentions, hidden stones and consent metadata. AsyncStorage is not app-encrypted.
  • Network pathways: typed city query to Open-Meteo with normal connection metadata/IP; UTC instant to snapshot endpoint; minimal birth inputs to full Natal endpoint only on saved permission plus explicit action. The server processes those calculation inputs; do not claim every calculation is on-device.
  • Application logging review confirms no raw natal request body is added to normal API logs; production hosting/proxy logs and their retention still need operator verification. Do not promise zero infrastructure logging or a deletion period without evidence.
  • Open-Meteo describes technical/IP logging and a 90-day log period in its own current policy. Link and attribute its policy; do not claim control of its retention.
  • No active profile sync, analytics, advertising or payment service. OS backup behavior is separate. Email support is optional and should not include birth data.
  • Terms decisions: symbolic/non-medical scope, calculation limitations, availability, age audience, permitted use, local-loss/no-sync expectations, applicable law and statutory rights. Obtain appropriate review before release; do not invent operator/jurisdiction details.
